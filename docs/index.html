AI Incident Radar

Aviation has accident investigators; AI mostly has news cycles. This pipeline merges the AI Incident Database, the AIAAIC Repository and Hacker News reports, deduplicates them with embeddings, classifies every incident with Gemini into the MIT AI Risk Repository domains plus safety failure modes, checks the classifier against human labels, and flags what is rising in the last 90 days. Refreshed weekly by GitHub Actions. Code & method.

In plain words

When a plane crashes, investigators record what went wrong so it never happens again. AI mostly gets headlines. This keeps the record.
